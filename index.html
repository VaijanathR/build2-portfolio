About

Vaijanath Prabhakar Ruge is a Quality Engineering and Delivery Management leader with 20+ years of global experience in Quality Assurance, Software Testing, and building Testing Centers of Excellence. He has built testing and BPO practices from scratch — growing one from 0 to 100+ testers within a year — while managing P&L and revenue targets of up to $7M per annum, with onsite experience across the USA, Canada, UK, Switzerland, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and Egypt.

He currently serves as Founding Partner and Technology Director at Effyklean Industries, and has previously held Vice-President and General Manager roles at MFX, Nous Infosystems, and Expleo Group, leading test automation, performance engineering, and digital transformation practices. His toolset spans Tricentis Tosca, Selenium, Playwright, JMeter, and Jenkins across the Banking, Insurance, Healthcare, Retail, and Manufacturing domains.
